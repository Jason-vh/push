Session page

A session reads like a match report. Your own result comes first: how many rating points you gained, how your rank moved, and your record. Below that come the session's standings and every match with what it did to your rating.

You're Jason. The numbers use the app's real Elo maths (K = 24, team rating = the pair's average).

States

    Decisions baked in

    • "Points" means rating points. The game score total (e.g. 31–24) sits in a tile next to it.
    • Standings are ordered by rating gained this session, not by wins, so a brave loss to a stronger pair still counts.
    • Every match shows what it did to your rating, or "Sat out".
    • Editing the session (date, time, players) stays behind the Edit button; deleting moves to the bottom.

    Open questions

    • Should others' rating changes be visible to everyone, or only your own?
    • Should a finished session lock after a day or so, so ratings don't shift later?
