    Decisions baked in

    • Availability is per day, with an optional note like "after 19:00". No time slots.
    • No legend. A day with anyone keen shows four slots that fill up, so "two of four" reads on its own. A booked day shows its start time instead.
    • Anyone past four goes on the bench, which you see when you open the day.
    • "Someone is booking" only shows in the day sheet, not on the calendar.
    • No claiming step. Whoever books a court with the club marks the day as booked. Who does the booking gets sorted in the group chat.
    • If more than four are keen, the booker picks the four who play.
    • Only the booker can edit or cancel a booking.
